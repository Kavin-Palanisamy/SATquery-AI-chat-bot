Working Capabilities

What SatQuery AI Can Do

Explore the four dedicated analysis studios designed for Earth Observation imagery.

💬

Ask Questions

What it does: Qualitative scene understanding, land-cover identification, and spatial feature reasoning without hallucinated numbers.

Input needed: 1 satellite image (Optical / GeoTIFF / PNG / JPEG)
Example question: "Describe the land cover and major objects visible in this image."
Try Ask Questions →
🎯

Find Objects

What it does: Pinpoints target features on the ground with precise bounding boxes and colorized segmentation masks.

Input needed: 1 satellite image (Optical / GeoTIFF / PNG / JPEG)
Example question: "Find and highlight the water body in this image."
Try Find Objects →
🔄

Before & After

What it does: Compares two dates of the same area to identify surface alteration, construction, or flood dynamics.

Input needed: 2 optical images (Before T1 + After T2)
Example question: "What changed between these two dates?"
Try Before & After →
🛰️

See Through Clouds

What it does: Cross-fuses optical spectral bands with microwave SAR radar backscatter to analyze terrain obscured by clouds.

Input needed: 1 Optical image + 1 SAR radar image
Example question: "Use optical and SAR together to identify built-up and water regions."
Try Cloud Penetration →
